Photosynthesis is the process by which plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to produce glucose and oxygen. It happens mainly in the chloroplasts. Chlorophyll absorbs light energy, which powers the chemical reactions that convert light energy into chemical energy. The light-dependent reactions produce ATP and NADPH, while the Calvin cycle uses them to build sugar molecules. Photosynthesis is essential because it supports plant growth and forms the base of most food chains. Without it, ecosystems would not have a reliable source of energy entering the food web.
